what's inside

compute
Orange Pi 5 Plus. Rockchip RK3588, 16GB RAM. Hardware h264 encode at 1080p60.
power
25,000mAh USB-C PD battery, 65W. Swappable mid-stream without dropping the feed.
modem
Internal 4G modem, single SIM, any carrier. A second USB port stays free for another modem.
antennas
Two external antennas routed up the shoulder straps.
storage
NVMe SSD. Records locally at full bitrate while the stream runs.
control
STREAM button on the left strap. One press starts, one press stops.
