privacy

This site collects close to nothing. Here is the whole list.

user@irlos:~$ cat PRIVACY.md
no tracking
No analytics, no ad pixels, no tracking cookies. The pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address when they load.
when you buy
Stripe processes the payment and shares your email, the item and the amount with this site. That is stored in an order database and used to deliver what you bought and answer support email. Card numbers never touch this site.
email
You get order confirmations and, for the backpack, ship updates. No marketing list. Replies go to me, not a ticket system.
server logs
The web server keeps standard access logs, IP address and requested page, for debugging and abuse prevention. They rotate out on a schedule.
deletion
Email me and I delete your order records, unless a payment dispute or tax law says they have to stay a while longer.
third parties
Stripe for payments and Google for fonts. That is the whole list.
