chat reader

Type a Kick channel, press play, lock your phone. The chat comes back as one continuous stream, so it keeps going with the screen off and the phone in your pocket.

Only the five most recent messages are held, so on a busy channel you hear what is being said now rather than falling further behind. Bot commands, links and repeated spam are skipped, and each person gets three lines every thirty seconds. Chat is read as it arrives: nothing is filtered for language.
